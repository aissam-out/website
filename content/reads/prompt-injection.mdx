Prompt injection is what happens when instructions and data share a channel. A webpage, email, or retrieved document can tell the model to ignore your system prompt, exfiltrate secrets, or abuse a tool.

Direct injection is the obvious jailbreak. Indirect injection is worse in products: the attack arrives inside content the system was supposed to trust as data. Once agents can browse, retrieve, or call APIs, that content becomes executable influence.

Guardrails are defense in depth, not a magic system message. Separate privileges for tools. Sanitize or isolate untrusted context. Require confirmation for irreversible actions. Log and rate-limit sensitive operations. Treat the model as a confused deputy, not as a security boundary.

If your only mitigation is “we told the model to be careful,” you have not secured the system. You have annotated it.
