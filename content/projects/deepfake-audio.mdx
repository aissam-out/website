[DeepFake-audio](https://github.com/aissam-out/DeepFake-audio) is a compact experiment: generate cloned speech clips.

I keep it here because conversational AI is not only “can the system talk?” It is also “should you believe the voice?” The same stack that makes assistants useful makes impersonation cheap.

Not a product. A working sketch, and a warning baked into the filename.
